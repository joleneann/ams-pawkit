Body · required
As Pune's monsoon settles in, we see a sharp rise in canine ear infections. Humid air + trapped moisture in the ear canal is a breeding ground for bacteria and yeast. The good news: most flares are easy to catch early if you check the ears weekly and dry them after walks or baths. Use a cotton ball (not Q-tips) to gently wipe the outer ear. If you notice redness, dark discharge, smell, or head-shaking, message us — we have a 7-day recheck protocol that works well.
Warning signs
  • Repeated head-shaking or scratching at one ear
  • Dark or smelly discharge from the ear canal
  • Visible redness or swelling around the ear opening
  • Whining or flinching when you touch the ear

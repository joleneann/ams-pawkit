# Photography direction

Pet photos are the emotional core of the parent app. Bad photos kill premium
feel even with perfect chrome. The Fernandes 4 plus 200 synthetics share one
visual standard.

## Composition

- **Aspect ratio:** 4:5 portrait crop (matches Pet Page 320×400 cover)
- **Subject placement:** pet centred horizontally; eyes at upper third
  (rule-of-thirds)
- **Pet-to-frame ratio:** 60% pet / 40% negative space. Never tighter
  (claustrophobic), never looser (lost subject).
- **Crop discipline:** never crop ears or whiskers; tail can leave the frame;
  lower body can crop at chest.

## Lighting

- **Source:** soft window light from camera-left at 45°
- **No flash, no clinical lighting, no on-camera fill**
- **Time of day:** 4-6pm Pune (golden hour adjacent, warm without orange
  cast)
- **Contrast target:** ratio 3:1 highlight-to-shadow (gentle modelling, not
  dramatic)

## Background

- **Setting:** neutral home environment, bedsheet, sofa arm, wood floor,
  plain wall.
- **Blur:** ~f/2.0-2.8 equivalent (Portrait mode on phone is sufficient).
- **Forbidden:** clinical settings (vet's exam table, kennel), busy
  patterns, identifiable family members, brand logos in frame.

## Pose discipline

- **Preferred:** alert, eye contact toward camera, mouth closed or relaxed.
- **Avoid:** mid-yawn, mid-bark, mid-groom, sleeping (memorial pets only
  get the sleeping exception).
- **Action shots:** no. This is editorial portrait, not documentary.

## Treatment

- **Warmth:** +10 (yellow shift; pulls slightly toward canvas warmth)
- **Saturation:** -5 (subtle desaturation reads editorial vs. amateur)
- **No filters, no presets, no AI enhancement**
- **Format:** JPEG quality 85 for storage; WebP for web delivery

## Synthetic 200 curation

Each of 200 synthetic pets gets one Pexels image matching the above.
Process:

1. Curated Pexels keyword sets per species/breed (e.g. "indie dog Pune home"
   for desi dogs; "white persian cat indoor" for white Persian)
2. Pre-filter 20-30 candidates per pet
3. Manual cull for lighting consistency (hardest filter; most Pexels pet
   stock is over-lit)
4. Final 1 per pet, replace bad finds during Day 8 rehearsal review

Estimated curation time: ~3h for 200 pets.

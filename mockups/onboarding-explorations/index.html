Onboarding · Phase 2

Four direction concepts for OnboardingHousehold + OnboardingFirstPet. Each opens a side-by-side render of both screens in that direction. Pick one to advance into the build.

D1 Linear-style · restrained

No Skip button (smart default if blank). Single full-width Continue. Minimum chrome, maximum whitespace. Anti-onboarding ethos: barely there.

Ref: Linear · Style: minimal-confident · Risk: cold

D2 Notion-style · warm illustrated

Soft mauve radial wash on the canvas. SVG paw-cluster illustration anchors the empty middle. Headline gains warmth via one Lora italic accent word ("household", "first pet").

Ref: Notion · Style: friendly-editorial · Risk: illustration must not feel AI-slop

D3 Stripe-style · editorial serif hero

Lora italic 600 at 40px takes the headline ("Welcome.", "Gabby."). Inter for everything else. The serif at scale IS the visual identity, no illustration needed. Magazine cover energy.

Ref: Stripe · Style: refined-magazine · Risk: expands Lora scope beyond pet names (worth re-locking in CLAUDE.md)

D4 Pet-app convention · photo-led

Step 1 stays compact. Step 2 leads with a 120px circular dashed photo placeholder at the top — "Add a photo of Gabby. We’ll find their fur palette from it." Pet photo is the centerpiece, fields support it.

Ref: Pawp / AirVet · Style: direct-conventional · Risk: convention can feel generic without strong typography to anchor

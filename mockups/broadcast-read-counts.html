Four metrics, not twenty Audience / Read / Rate / Care-kit views. Anything more is dashboard bloat and Sagar doesn't need it. Care kit views is muted because v0 only tracks broadcast taps, not deep clicks into the attached kit (v1+).
Time curve Hour-by-hour reads after send. The seed staggers 70% of reads in the first 6h and 30% in the remaining 18h, producing the spike-then-taper shape vets will recognize from real consumer messaging tools.

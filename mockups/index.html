Brand system (current, mauve-only v1.4)

Palette. Canvas #F8F7F5, rail-tint ground #EFE6E8, Ink #0F0C0A, Ink-soft, Ink-faint #8F8B86, 10 fur tokens (Milk to Sable), Boysenberry #9C2B5C as the brand amplifier (Berry-deep #7C1F47 + Berry-soft 12% tint + Berry-ring 30% focus).

Type. Inter for body + display, Lora italic 600 for pet names in hero contexts only. Noto Sans Devanagari for Marathi.

Icons. Phosphor filled (@phosphor-icons/react) with a global weight: "fill" default.

Discipline. No hand-rolled UI primitives anywhere; every interactive element is a shadcn primitive. Type sizes come from the Pawkit scale (text-xxs through text-display); arbitrary text-[Npx] values are banned. Pure white and pure black forbidden. Fur tokens never in chrome. No em dashes anywhere.

Pet Page as hero. The per-pet profile is the editorial centerpiece of the parent app and the demo's emotional center. Fur-match is a secondary visual delight inside the hero, not the centerpiece.

What's pitched

Two apps, one design system. Next.js dashboard for the clinic + Expo Android app for parents, delivered as an EAS release APK to the demo phone.

25 to 30 minute live demo. Laptop dashboard + phone parent app. 15 minutes of app demo + 10 to 15 minutes of growth pitch. The parent app serves low-literacy users (about 20% of AMS pet parents) with bilingual EN+MR, icon-led design, large tap targets.

Real Fernandes pets. Raffy (memorial), Gabby, Angel, Galaxy. Their fur-match results are pre-locked from real photos.

AI scope is strictly minimum. Two features, both Sarvam-vendor, both dashboard-only, both producing text Sagar reviews before output ships: Sarvam Audio (voice INPUT to text on dashboard compose surfaces) and Sarvam Translate (auto EN ↔ MR at compose time, vet reviews + approves sequentially). No AI on parent app.

Three engines for ARR. Clinic SaaS (anchor), parent subscription (Pawkit Plus), and clinically-curated marketplace. ₹20cr ARR forecast in 3 years; each engine can stand alone if one underperforms.

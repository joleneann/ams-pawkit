Phase 2 · Screen 07b · Settings
Three settings directions on the locked chrome
Reached from the Settings rail item. Header search bar hidden per Section 1 spec (Settings has no searchable content). Single-column scrollable page. Four content blocks: Profile (photo, full name, vet license, email, all inline-editable), Account (read-only phone tied to auth, EN/MR chrome-language toggle), Clinic info (hard-coded AMS Pune in v0, no edit affordance), Sign out (Ink ghost, no Teal). Quiet Pawkit v0.1 vendor mark at the bottom is the only place Pawkit-as-vendor appears in the dashboard chrome (white-label discipline per docs/decisions-log.md).
Reading the three directions. Same chrome (Settings rail active, search bar hidden). Same content (Profile, Account, Clinic, Sign out, EN+MR toggle). Each direction renders the same fields with a different visual treatment per docs/layout-spec.md Section 7b's locked Steal anchors: Linear settings density, Stripe profile screen rhythm, Notion small-caps section labels. The variance is stage width, card chrome vs none, profile-photo prominence, Edit-affordance style, and sign-out placement.
Direction A
Linear-channeled · dense inline-edit, hairlines only
720px stage. No card chrome around sections; small-caps section labels sit above hairline 1px Ink-faint dividers between rows. 160px label column + value flex + small "Edit" pill on the right (Ink-faint border, 3px radius). Profile photo at 48px in a top photo-row with thin top + bottom borders, "Upload" pill on the right. EN/MR pill toggle sits inline as a normal field value, with helper text below pulled to the 176px indent. Clinic info renders as plain prose lines (no tinted block, no border). Sign out as full-width ghost button at the bottom. Quiet Pawkit v0.1 mark centred below a hairline divider. Reads as a working surface, not a display.

Pawkit · v0.1
Teal
One Teal: rail Settings-active 4px left border. No CTA on this surface (Settings is configuration, not action; one-Teal-per-screen rule honoured by the rail border alone).
Steal · Pitfall
Steal Linear's settings density: hairline-only dividers, inline-edit pills, no card chrome. Pitfall: density can read as flat or unloved if hierarchy isn't carried by the small-caps section labels. Mitigation: 36px gap between sections + tiered type rhythm (Satoshi page title, small-caps labels, Inter rows) carry the structure without cards.
Risk
Inline-edit pills next to every row may feel busy at first glance. Counter: pills are tiny (4/10 padding, Ink-faint border, Ink-soft text), they read as scannable action targets rather than chrome noise once Sagar uses the page twice.
Direction B
Stripe-channeled · cards, profile photo prominent
760px stage. Each section sits in its own card (1px Ink-faint border, 8px radius, 22/26px padding). The Profile card opens with a hero photo row: 72px circular avatar slot + name in Satoshi 17pt + "Animal Medical Services" affiliation in Ink-soft below + "Upload photo" Ink-outline button on the right. Field rows below get more vertical breathing (14px padding) and a slightly larger value type (14pt). Edit affordances are plain text links with a thin Ink-faint underline, no border (Stripe pattern). Clinic info is its own card matching the rest. Sign out lives in a final card with helper text on the left and a compact right-aligned ghost button. Vendor mark sits centred below all cards with no divider.

Pawkit · v0.1
Teal
One Teal: rail Settings-active border, same as Direction A. Cards add no Teal accents.
Steal · Pitfall
Steal Stripe's profile-card rhythm: hero photo row at the top of Profile, descriptive sub under each card title, plain underline Edit links. Pitfall: 8px-radius cards on cream Paper can drift toward generic-SaaS if borders read too crisp. Mitigation: 1px Ink-faint border (not Ink) keeps cards quiet, the photo hero carries warmth.
Risk
Four stacked cards may feel heavier than Settings deserves (only 6 editable fields total). Counter: cards give the photo-hero room to breathe and let the Clinic block feel like a verifiable identity card (which matches its function as the AMS letterhead source).
Direction C
Notion-channeled · narrow stage, small-caps lead
640px stage, narrower than Linear or Stripe. Small-caps section labels lead the structural rhythm with a thin 1px Ink-faint underline beneath each label. Field rows are dashed-divider light. Profile photo at 40px reads as auxiliary metadata at the top of the Profile section, not a hero. Edit affordances as quiet pencil ghosts on the right (no text "Edit" label, no border). Clinic info as plain prose without card chrome. Sign out as small inline ghost button (no full-width, no card). Reads as a quiet document, not a control panel. Best fit if Sagar visits Settings rarely.

Pawkit · v0.1
Teal
One Teal: rail Settings-active border, same as A and B. Pencil-ghost edits stay Ink-faint until hover.
Steal · Pitfall
Steal Notion's small-caps section labels with a thin bottom rule + dashed-divider quiet rows + pencil-ghost edits. Pitfall: too quiet can make edit affordances un-discoverable for low-literacy or first-time users. Mitigation: hover state on each row reveals the pencil at Ink, and the row tints subtly to telegraph "this is editable".
Risk
Pencil-only edits with no text label fail accessibility / discoverability for users who don't recognise the icon. Counter: Settings is admin-only (Sagar, not parents). The wider parent app uses Pencil sparingly. Sagar uses Settings <1x/week so a quieter density is appropriate; the cost of low affordance is low here.

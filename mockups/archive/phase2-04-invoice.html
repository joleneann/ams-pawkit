Strand A · Design references · UI taste
Four cross-industry invoice / statement benchmarks
Stripe Invoicing
Steal: grouped invoice anatomy with category headers ("Blue items / Red items / Other"), per-group subtotal stack, top-level Summary block. Exactly the per-category section Pawkit needs. Also the always-visible info-icon pattern next to line names.
Pulley
Steal: cream/paper background and editorial tabular columns. Muted olive/sage status pills, calm typographic rhythm. Tone-matches Paper + Ink palette almost directly. Plain-English label voice on jargon-heavy concepts.
Ramp
Steal: 2026 Budget overview pattern. Category-rolled-up line items with section name + tabular columns (Total, Actuals, Committed, Remaining), thin underline-style utilization bars, clean editorial type on warm off-white. Heavy on hierarchy without ornament.
Brex Bill Pay
Steal: table with vendor avatars (here = category-color dots), monospace tabular amounts, clear status pills ("Scheduled" / "Ready for payment") in tabular layout. Most scannable list pattern of the four refs.
Strand B · Category competitors · vet / healthcare billing
Three category benchmarks (PetDesk, Weave, Vetsource)
PetDesk
Note: PetDesk publishes no finished-invoice screenshot publicly. Closest billing surface is a "Send an invoice" modal over the messaging inbox. Useful as authentic vet-clinic context. Pawkit does NOT have a Send invoice CTA (no Mark paid, no Send to family); admin invoice is view-only.
Weave Payments
Steal: Take Payment modal with named patient header, location pill, info-icon next to a charge toggle ("Include Surcharge"), and a clean Totals row. Validates the info-icon-on-charge-line pattern + the parallel-totals stack we already plan.
Vetsource
Note: Vetsource publishes no public invoice or order-detail UI. This is a marketing composite (real Pharmacy Dashboard metrics visible inside a stock photo with watercolor overlay). Used only as evidence of brand tone (clean white surfaces, orange accent), not as a structural reference for Pawkit Screen 04.

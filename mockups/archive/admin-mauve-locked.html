Title
Monsoon ear-infection watch for dogs
Summary
·Pune monsoon is here; humid weather flares ear infections in dogs.
·Check ears weekly, dry them after walks, and watch for head-shaking.
·Floppy-eared breeds (Goldens, Spaniels, Labs) are at higher risk.
Body
As Pune's monsoon settles in, we see a sharp rise in canine ear infections. Humid air + trapped moisture in the ear canal is a breeding ground for bacteria and yeast. The good news: most flares are easy to catch early if you check the ears weekly and dry them after walks or baths. Use a cotton ball (not Q-tips) to gently wipe the outer ear. If you notice redness, dark discharge, smell, or head-shaking, bring them in — we have a 7-day recheck protocol that works well.
Warning signs
  • Repeated head-shaking or scratching at one ear
  • Dark or smelly discharge from the ear canal
  • Visible redness or swelling around the ear opening
  • Whining or flinching when you touch the ear
When to call us

Message us through Pawkit if you see any of the warning signs above — Dr Sagar will get back to you within an open follow-up window.

Call the clinic line (+91 20 2612 3456) for after-hours urgent issues. We are walk-in Mon–Sat 9am–9pm.

Fraunces

italic 600, SOFT axis = 100 · most distinctive

Modern variable serif with a literal SOFT axis. At full softness, terminals round and the letterforms gain personality. Most "Pawkit-ish" of the four — pairs naturally with warm pet content. Slight risk of feeling quirky / boutique at hero size.

How to choose. Look at the inbox row and billing row sizes first — those are the surfaces you'll see hundreds of times. The hero size matters less because it appears once per page. Ask: at 15–17px, does the italic feel quiet and warm, or does it still grab the eye?

My read. Fraunces (A) has the most personality — could feel right for a pet-clinic brand if the soft axis lands. Newsreader (B) is the safest text-friendly pick. Crimson Pro (C) is the most "thoughtful book" of the four. Lora (D) is the least distinctive but reads cleanest in dense rows.

Steal · Pitfall
Steal Mailchimp's "Filter for [chip]" row pattern + HubSpot's add-filter dashed CTA. Pitfall: every filter row sits at the same visual weight, harder to spot which filter is doing the most narrowing. Mitigation: the count strip and "94% of AMS dog parents" line do the narrowing math out loud.
Risk
Chip-per-value gets crowded if a filter has many options (e.g., chronic conditions list). v0 filters are small (species 3 options · deceased toggle · age input), so this scales for now.
Direction B
Customer.io-channeled · hero count + sample preview
Filter controls compact in a sidebar (280px) on the left. Right side is dominated by a big-number hero ("187 pets · 1.8% of clinic") and a sample preview panel showing 4 actual pets who match the filter (named, with avatars). This is the "trust" direction: Sagar sees exactly who he's sending to before pressing Publish, not just an abstract count.

Direction D
Weave / Beehiiv-channeled · toggle-driven filter cards
Each filter is a card with a toggle. Toggle on = filter applies; toggle off = filter ignored. Cards expand to reveal the value picker when toggled on. Most beginner-friendly of the four; lowest cognitive load. The audience count sits at the bottom as a 2px Ink-bordered totals row (same visual pattern as the Screen 04 invoice total).

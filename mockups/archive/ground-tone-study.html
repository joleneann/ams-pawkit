How to choose. Look at each ground for ~5 seconds. Notice: (1) does the page feel emotionally warm? (2) does the canvas card still "lift" off the ground? (3) does the berry chip + berry brand sit comfortably or fight the ground?

Tell me which option (A / B / C / D) you want — or if none feel right, what direction to push next (less saturation, different hue, higher contrast, etc).

Implementation will be a one-line change in apps/dashboard/app/globals.css — replace --rail-tint from the current 4.5% ink wash to an opaque hex matching your pick.

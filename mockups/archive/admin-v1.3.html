Screen 06

Health Kit editor - educational content (locked May 4)

Health Kit = educational content the vet authors for pet parents (e.g. "How to care for your dog in the summer"), NOT a service bundle. Sections: title + key_points + body + warning_signs + escalation, all bilingual EN+MR. Cover image optional. Each published kit gets a public URL (pawkit.app/kits/[slug]) so parents can share to non-Pawkit users via WhatsApp / Social / Email - the virality layer. Publish is the single Teal CTA.

Title
ENMR
How to care for your dog in the summer
Key points (bullet summary)
ENMR
· Provide unlimited fresh water - change it twice daily
· Protect their paws - asphalt above 50°C burns paw pads in seconds
· NEVER leave them in a parked car, even with windows cracked
· Walk before 7 am or after 7 pm only
· Recognise heat stroke: panting, drooling, vomiting, collapse
Warning signs ●
Excessive panting that doesn't slow with rest. Thick drool. Vomiting. Bright red gums. Stumbling or collapse. Body temperature above 39.5°C measured rectally.
Escalation - when to call us ●
If rectal temperature exceeds 39.5°C OR your dog cannot stand, this is an emergency. Call AMS immediately at +91-2026123456. While en route, wet your dog with cool (NOT cold) water and run AC at full blast.

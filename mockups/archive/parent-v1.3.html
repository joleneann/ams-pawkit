Repositioning vs v1.2 + Transformed refinement (2026-05-10)

  • Was hero, now delight. The brand hero is the steady-state Pet Page (Screen 02). Fur-match three-state is a secondary moment of delight inside that hero. NOT the demo wow, NOT the centerpiece, NOT the screenshottable moment. Demonstrable in the demo, lives quietly inside the parent app the rest of the time.
  • Transformed state refined 2026-05-10. Previously showed a full Honey→Peach gradient cover that the parent never actually sees in steady-state (the locked Pet Page is real photo + 10px accent stripe). Now the Transformed state mirrors steady-state: real photo at 280px, the 10px Honey accent border draws in with a small animation, and the magic toast is the only ephemeral overlay. Toast dismisses in 4-5 seconds, page resolves into the locked steady-state. Promise lined up with payoff; no design lie.
  • Magic toast microcopy reframed - from tech-magic ("Algorithm complete!") to care-driven ("A palette as unique as she is."). Final EN + MR strings deferred to microcopy round.
  • Override picker (Screen 04) reachable from the Transformed toast (one-time) AND from Per-pet Settings (Screen 13) any time after.
  • After this flow runs once, every subsequent visit to Gabby's page is the steady-state Pet Page (Screen 02), with the algorithm result preserved as the 10px Honey accent border at the cover bottom.
  • Where fur-match still lives across the app (so the ceremony introduces a system, not a one-off visual): avatar fur-match rings on Pets tab list, Inbox per-pet rows, master Settings Pets section; the memorial card for deceased pets (preserves the gradient at 60% opacity with Heart icon); the Pawkit app icon (Honey to Peach derived from Gabby's match, frozen as the brand signature); the splash screen extending that gradient; the Pet Page 10px bottom accent.

Override choreography

  • 75% sheet height, drag handle. Triggered by tapping the colour badge on Transformed Pet Page (opt-in, not forced).
  • Two-tone hidden under "More options" - deviation from earlier always-visible spec. Most parents only need solo tone; reduces noise.
  • 3×3+1 chip grid, each chip = one fur token. Selected chip gets 3px Ink ring (here Honey).
  • Named save button - "Save Honey →" (or "Save Honey + Peach" for two-tone). Naming the action makes the override feel intentional, not accidental.
  • "Try a new photo" link at bottom - for re-sampling when the original photo was bad.
  • Algorithm vs user_choice diff is captured in the schema (algorithm_match_* + user_override_at) for v2 model training.

Per-pet thread architecture (locked 2026-05-08)

  • One row per living pet. Gabby (open follow-up window, unreplied parent-side message). Angel (window closed, last item is a system reminder). Galaxy (no conversations yet, empty-state subline).

Why a separate tab

  • Mode separation. 1:1 vet-parent conversations (Inbox) and clinic-wide one-way broadcasts (Announcements) are different communication modes. Mixing them in one feed was poor UX (locked retired 2026-05-08).
  • Health Kits live here. A kit on the Announcements feed is just an announcement with an attached kit. Tap card → Screen 06 reading view.
  • Composer disabled. One-way feed; parent reads, no reply path. Per locked discipline, parent app accepts no incoming messages outside an open follow-up window, and announcements aren't follow-ups.
  • Per-card EN/MR toggle for body content (parent's UI language stays separate). Read-state persists across devices via the `broadcasts_read` table (Schema Patch 7).

Master Settings architecture (locked 2026-05-09)

  • 6th tab, icon-only. Settings sits at the rightmost tab position. All 6 tabs render as icons without text labels (locked 2026-05-09); active state is icon Ink + stroke-width 2; inactive is Ink-faint + stroke-width 1.75. Aria-labels carry the role for screen readers.

  • Sign out. Ghost button at the bottom, full-width, Ink border + Ink text. Confirmation flow on tap, microcopy in microcopy round.
  • Deferred to v1+ (recorded 2026-05-09): per-pet or per-type notification granularity; privacy / data export / account deletion; add-household-member / multi-user invites; help / FAQ / contact (the closed-thread redirect carries clinic phone for now); theme / dark mode; pet-level edits surfaced here (those live on Per-pet Settings); memorial-mode editing for deceased pets.

Per-pet Settings architecture (locked 2026-05-09)

  • Two entry points. Gear pill on Pet Page cover (Screen 02 top-right) AND the Pets section row on master Settings (Screen 12). Both navigate to this same sub-screen.

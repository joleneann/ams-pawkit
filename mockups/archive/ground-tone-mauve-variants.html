Tuning the mauve ground

You picked Option B (Mauve wash). The risk: berry-soft accents (picker chip, Outstanding KPI, unpaid status pills) might compete with a mauve ground instead of standing out against it. Three sub-variants below test mitigations. Each renders the Billing screen — the densest berry-accent stack on the dashboard — so you can see exactly how the competition plays out.

Three mitigations on the table

  • 1. Desaturate the ground. Pull the ground hue toward warm-gray (less explicitly pink). Same warmth, less direct competition with berry-soft accents. Lowest effort.
  • 2. Reinforce the accents. Stronger borders on the Outstanding KPI + a thin ring on the unpaid status pill. Makes berry-soft accents read as "defined objects" not "wash".
  • 3. Both at once. Tune the ground AND reinforce accents. Strongest separation but highest visual change.
Jump to B1. Original mauve · #EFE5E8 B2. Desaturated · #ECE7E8 B3. Cooler lavender tilt · #EBE5EA B4. B2 + reinforced accents

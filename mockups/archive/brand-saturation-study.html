Brand saturation study · 2026-05-15

Push the berry and the ground out of the gray zone

The current setup reads dusty for two reasons. (1) Berry primary #823159 sits at ~45% saturation in HSL — it's a "muted Boysenberry", not a vivid one. (2) Ground #ECE7E8 is so close to neutral that the eye still reads it as gray, even though there's a faint mauve hint. Both contribute to the page reading desaturated.

Four options below push both at once — a punchier berry paired with a ground that has visible chroma. The Boysenberry hue family is preserved across A/B/C/D (no pivot to a different brand color). Option D is the "stay berry, break the pink monoculture" move with a warm-cream ground instead. Current baseline is shown at the top.

Jump to Current A. Tasteful pop B. Magenta-punch C. Modern vibrant D. Berry + warm cream

What changes between options

Each variant updates --primary (the berry) AND --rail-tint (the ground) together. --berry-soft (12% alpha) and --berry-ring (28% alpha) are derived from the new berry automatically, so the active Inbox rail item, Outstanding KPI, unpaid pills and active tab fill all shift in concert. The canvas card color #F8F7F5 never changes.

How to read them. Look at three things on each option: (1) does the page feel warmer / more alive than the baseline? (2) is the berry accent ("Awaiting reply" pill, Start reply queue CTA, clinic mark) confident or still dusty? (3) does the active Inbox rail item still read distinct from the ground?

My read. A is the smallest tasteful upgrade — keeps Boysenberry identity, removes the gray. B adds a little magenta confidence without losing the family. D is the one that delivers the biggest "less gray" feel because the ground is a completely different hue from the berry — the contrast does the heavy lifting. C is the most aggressive — only pick this if you want to leave the Boysenberry name behind.

If none feel right. Tell me which way to push next: different hue family (peach? coral? plum?), keep berry but push the ground further from pink, or different saturation balance. I'll build another round.

Implementation, once chosen: edits to --rail-tint, --primary (HSL), --berry-deep, --berry-soft, --berry-ring in apps/dashboard/app/globals.css, plus token mirrors in packages/design-tokens/src/tokens.css.

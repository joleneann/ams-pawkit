Body
Maharashtra summers are tough on dogs. Their bodies don't sweat like ours and they can overheat quickly, especially short-nosed breeds (pugs, bulldogs) and senior dogs. This guide covers the basics of keeping your dog safe through May to August.
Warning signs
  • Excessive panting that doesn't slow with rest
  • Thick drool
  • Vomiting
  • Bright red gums
  • Stumbling or collapse
  • Body temperature above 39.5°C measured rectally
Escalation · when to call us

If rectal temperature exceeds 39.5°C OR your dog cannot stand, this is an emergency.

Call AMS immediately at +91 20 2612 3456.

While en route, wet your dog with cool (NOT cold) water and run AC at full blast.

AMS hours: 9am to 9pm, Monday to Saturday.

The 7 components, sketched

Visual reference for the components added under the Premium Feel System (Branch B). Each section shows the component rendered, where it appears, and notes on the discipline. This is what to build in Figma before designing the 14 screens. Total work is about 6.5 hours.

01 · 1 hour to build

Loading Skeletons

Grey placeholder shapes that match the layout of the content about to load. Replace spinning wheels everywhere. Stillness reads as confidence. The shape of what's coming reduces perceived wait time.

How it behaves. Skeleton fades in 200ms after the data request fires (the 200ms delay prevents a flash on fast networks). When real content arrives, it cross-fades in over 250ms. Never a sudden swap. Solid Ink-faint at 50% opacity. No shimmer (the moving sparkle effect feels cheap).

02 · 30 minutes to build

Empty States

What the screen shows when there is nothing yet. One italic sentence, no illustrations, no "Get started" buttons. Treat absence as calm, not as a missed opportunity to upsell.

The discipline. Same component everywhere, only the text changes. Satoshi 16pt italic in Ink-faint, single sentence ending in a period. No exclamation marks. The admin inbox copy is slightly warmer because it talks to the vet, who carries cognitive load. Parent copy is matter of fact.

03 · 1 hour to build

Error states (3 flavours)

Three different ways to surface errors depending on context. All share the same look (Paper card, 1.5px Ink border, 4px Ink left bar plus a Lucide AlertCircle icon in the headline) but live in different places and behave differently. Fur tokens never in chrome (locked 2026-05-08); alert signal is structural and iconographic, not chromatic.

Flavour 1 · Inline form error (under a single field)

Email

That email doesn't look right.

When. User typed something invalid in a form field. The field's bottom border thickens to 2px Ink (instead of the default 1px Ink-faint) and the message sits below in italic. Stays visible until the field is corrected. Fur tokens never in chrome (locked 2026-05-08); alert signal is heavier underline weight, no colour.

Flavour 2 · Banner error (top of screen, blocking)

Couldn't send broadcast. Check your connection and try again. Retry

When. Something blocked a primary action. Sits at the top of the affected screen, persists until the user dismisses it or successfully retries. Includes a Retry link.

Flavour 3 · Toast error (slides up from bottom, transient)

Couldn't upload the photo. Try again? ×

When. Something failed in the background but isn't blocking. Slides in from the bottom of the screen, auto-dismisses after 6 seconds. User can also close it with the ×. Used for upload failures, save errors, etc.

Why never red. Red is the brand colour of generic SaaS errors. Pawkit uses Peach (a warm fur-palette token, alert role only) for all error chrome. It signals concern without alarm. Care-driven, not panic-driven.

Offline Banner

Thin Paper banner at the top of any screen when the phone is offline. Reassures the parent that nothing is broken. Pawkit works mostly offline (fur-match runs on the device), so this is a calm message, not an error.

The behaviour. Banner slides down from the top in 250ms when the device goes offline. Slides up and away when connection restores. Pending messages get a "Will send when online" footnote. Inbox shows last-fetched data with a freshness caption. Italic Ink-soft 12pt, never red, never alarming.

05 · 1 hour to build

Vet Byline

How Dr Sagar's name and credentials appear across the app. Treated like a magazine byline (think New York Times article credits), not a profile chip. The most-repeated premium signal in the app.

Variant 1 · Compact (one line, horizontal)

Dr Sagar Bhongale·Animal Medical Services

Used in. Inbox row meta, conversation thread headers, broadcast cards. Inter 13pt semibold for the name, middle dot in Ink-faint, Inter 11pt small caps Ink-soft for the clinic name. All on one line; clinic truncates with ellipsis if width is tight.

Variant 2 · Full (two lines stacked)

Dr Sagar Bhongale

Animal Medical Services

Used on. Pet Page cover and Health Kit reading view header. Name in Inter 14pt semibold, clinic below in Inter 11pt small caps Ink-soft.

Variant 3 · Centre (under Health Kit cover)

Dr Sagar Bhongale

Animal Medical Services

Used on. Health Kit reading view, centred under the cover image. Same as Full variant but centre-aligned with 16px top margin from cover bottom.

Why one component, three variants. Build once in Figma using the Variants feature. When the build agents implement it, the same component file (VetByline.tsx) shows up in 4+ places with the right look per context. Hard rule: never wraps to 3 lines. Credential format locked 2026-05-09: name + clinic only. No school, no graduation year, no license number on the byline; that detail belongs to a future "About this vet" surface, not the recurring byline. Marathi version uses "डॉ. सागर भोंगळे · ॲनिमल मेडिकल सर्व्हिसेस" with the same composition (final MR translation in microcopy round).

06 · 2 hours to build

App Icon

The icon Pawkit shows on Sagar's home screen and app drawer. Type-only mark: lowercase "p" in Satoshi semibold, centred in an Ink rounded square with a tiny Teal dot at the bowl of the letter (visible at 64px and larger only). Designed once at 1024px, exported to all sizes from one source.

Why type-only. No paw, no pet silhouette, no abstract shape. Pawkit is a typographic brand and the icon should survive at 1cm² on Sagar's home screen. Most app icons fail at that size because they have too much detail. The 22% corner radius matches the curve iOS and Android use to mask icons automatically. The Teal dot is the same brand mark dot from the dashboard header, miniaturised, and disappears at 32px because it would be muddy. Notification icon is monochrome (Android renders this in white in the status bar).

07 · 1 hour to build

Splash Screen

The screen Sagar sees for ~1 to 2 seconds when he taps the Pawkit icon, before the app's actual UI loads. Sets the brand tone every time he opens the app. Quiet and intentional, not a loading screen.

Splash · Paper bg, no spinner

Why no spinner. Stillness reads premium. The splash auto-dismisses the moment the app's JavaScript bundle loads (about 1 to 2 seconds on a mid-range Android). A spinner would say "we don't know how long this will take." A still wordmark says "we're ready." The Teal dot beside the "t" mirrors the same lockup in the dashboard header, so both apps feel like one brand. Spectral italic for the tagline because it's a warm phrase about the pet, even though no specific pet name appears here.

How to use this sheet. Open in Figma alongside this reference. Build each component as a Figma component (right-click frame, "Create component") so it can be reused across the 14 screens you'll design next. The 4 loading skeletons share building blocks (rectangles + circles in Ink-faint). The 3 error states share the same Paper card + Peach bar treatment. The vet byline uses Figma Variants to switch between the 3 sizes from one component. Total build time about 6.5 hours of focused Figma work.
